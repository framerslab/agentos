Class LibraryIndex

A library's sources in one collection of a vector store. Every passage of a source carries the source's tenant, access groups, kind, folder, tags and title in its metadata, so the store's own filter selects who sees it and the index checks what the store answers: indexSource replaces a source whole, setSourceScope changes who may see it and where it is filed, removeSource and removeTenant delete, and search reads only what its scope may see.

planSource

  • planSource(source, options): Promise<LibrarySourcePlan>

  • Plans indexing a source by content, without writing anything. Each passage's metadata gains inputHash (the SHA-256 of the model, the dimensions and its input), textHash (the SHA-256 of its text), embedModel and embedDimensions. A passage takes the vector of a stored passage of the source with the same inputHash: when the text is the same too, the row is kept and its metadata rewritten only where it differs (its place, title, folder, tags, access groups); when only the input is the same (two texts that redact to one input), the row keeps its vector and its text and metadata are rewritten, so a lexical search never finds the old words. A stored row without an inputHash, or embedded by another model or size, counts as absent. Other passages get new rows, <sourceId>#<newId()>, and their inputs go in toEmbed; stored rows no passage takes are removed.

    On a store without listSourcePassages, patchPassages and withSourceLock, every passage is embedded and the source is replaced whole when the plan is applied.

    Parameters

    • source: LibrarySource
    • options: LibraryPlanOptions

    Returns Promise<LibrarySourcePlan>

    Throws

    When the source has no tenant or no group, when there is not one input per passage, when the embedding has no model, a size that is not a whole number, or a size other than dimensions, or when newId gives an id twice, the id of a stored row, or a suffix holding a #.

applySourcePlan

  • applySourcePlan(plan, vectors): Promise<LibraryPlanCounts>

  • Writes a plan, with one vector for each input of its toEmbed, in order. Run it inside the host's storage transaction (an index over a view from PostgresVectorStore.withStorageTransaction) when other writes must commit with it. It takes the store's lock on the source, reads the source's rows again and throws LibraryPlanStale, writing nothing, when they are not the rows the plan was made from; then it removes, rewrites and adds rows, batchSize new rows to an upsert. Unchanged rows are not written. On a store that cannot plan by content it replaces the source whole, as indexSource does.

passageVectors

  • passageVectors(ids, scope, embedding): Promise<Map<string, number[]>>

  • The stored vectors of the passages among ids that the scope may see and that the model and size made, by id. The store reads them with the scope, the model and the size in its own filter (its optional fetchVectors), and the index checks each row again, so a passage of another tenant, outside the scope's groups, not active, or of another model never answers. On a store without fetchVectors the answer is empty.

indexSource

  • indexSource(source): Promise<{
        passages: number;
    }>

  • Indexes a source, replacing whatever the collection held under its id. Every passage is embedded first, batchSize at a time, so a failed embedding leaves the stored source as it was. Then, under the store's lock on the source where it has one (in one transaction on PostgresVectorStore), the old passages are deleted and the new ones written batchSize at a time, so a plan applied meanwhile is replaced whole. On a store without transactions a failed batch leaves the passages written before it, and indexing the source again replaces them. A delete or a write the store reports as failed in its result, instead of throwing, fails the call as well.

search

  • search(query): Promise<LibraryPassage[]>

  • Searches what the scope may see. The store's filter selects the passages, and the index keeps only those whose metadata meets the scope and the narrowing: a store whose filter drops or ignores a condition cannot widen the answer, which can then hold fewer than topK passages. With anyOf, each narrowing is searched leg by leg under the same scope and the answers are ranked as one search's (LibrarySearch.anyOf). Once its signal has aborted, a search starts no further call to the embedder or the store and rejects (LibrarySearch.signal).

searchGroups

  • searchGroups(query): Promise<LibraryGroupAnswer>

  • One search over the caller's own item lists, in named groups, on the store's lexical index, with no embedding. Each group is searched on its own, in the order handed, inside the scope and the items the caller hands for it and nowhere else, and answers:

    • total: the count of its distinct items that match every word, whatever the page. An item matches when every word matches in at least one of its passages, the same passage or different ones: the same word, a word it begins, or, under typo: 'one-edit', a stored word of the group's own passages one edit away.
    • hits: a page of those items, each by its best passage, in the order of order, with the words marked.
    • cursor: what the next page takes. It is bound to the words, the order, the profile, the part kinds, the typo rule and its limit, the group and the stored words the first page found, which a later page matches again and never reads anew, and it keeps the group's fingerprint and the scope: a later page handed another fingerprint, or asked under another tenant or other access groups, answers restart, so that every item is listed once across the pages of one unchanged list.
    • anyWord: on a first page of two words or more with no item that holds them all, the items that hold some.
    • incomplete: whether a word had more stored words one edit away than the limit, or the signal stopped the read.

    With no word in text, each group lists every item that holds a passage, a passage of one of partKinds where the group names any, in the date order, each by its first such passage.

    The index checks each passage the store answers against the scope, the group's items and its part kinds, and a search whose store answers a passage outside them fails: its count would hold what the scope does not.

    Parameters

    • query: LibraryGroupSearch

    Returns Promise<LibraryGroupAnswer>

    Throws

    When the scope has no tenant or no group, when no group is handed, when a part of the search is not in the form LibraryGroupSearch gives it, when the store has no lexicalGroupSearch, and a LibraryCursorError for a cursor the index did not make or that was made for another search: one whose group is not handed, whose words, order, profile, part kinds, typo rule or expansion limit differ, that carries a stored word no word of the search reaches or more stored words than one tsquery holds, or whose key names no place in the order (a rank past what a Postgres numeric holds, a date that is not a real instant, a source id holding NUL).
