sendAudio

  • sendAudio(chunk): Promise<void>

  • Plays one chunk; completion means its carrier checkpoint returned before an interruption or a close.

    A chunk with a generation is one producer's at a time: a second chunk before the first completes is refused, and so are an older generation and, once a generation was sent, a chunk without one. A chunk without a generation from a producer that sends none, as the voice pipeline without a call profile sends none and does not wait for one send before the next, is admitted behind the chunks before it: each is written in order with its checkpoint after it, without waiting for the earlier chunks to play, and at most 196608 bytes of their PCMU wait unwritten. An interruption rejects every chunk not yet played; the chunks sent after it play. A chunk outside TelephonyAudioChunk, such as one in an MP3 or Ogg container, is refused before output.

    Parameters

    • chunk: EncodedAudioChunk

    Returns Promise<void>
