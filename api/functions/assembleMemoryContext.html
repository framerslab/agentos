Function assembleMemoryContext

  • assembleMemoryContext(input): AssembledMemoryContext

  • Assemble memory context into a single formatted string within the given token budget.

    The preamble's tokens come off the budget first, then an involuntary memory's (at most INVOLUNTARY_MAX_SHARE of the rest) when a host passes the mechanisms engine. Each section is then granted its share of what remains (allocation over DEFAULT_BUDGET_ALLOCATION, where a key set to undefined or null keeps its default; shares that are not finite positive numbers count as 0, and shares adding up to more than 1 are scaled down), capped at what its content needs. A section whose share is set to exactly 0 is off: it gets no grant, takes no spill and is left out.

    The first pass goes through the sections in MEMORY_BUDGET_SPILL_ORDER: each renders on its grant plus whatever the sections before it left unspent (the shares of empty or under-filled sections, the rounding, and any share left unallocated). The second pass, in the same order, gives what is still unspent (a section that could not use its grant, such as working memory too long for it) to the sections with content left out, so the block can fill the whole budget. The second pass only adds to the block: it adds items the first pass left out at their rank, when they now fit whole, adds lines to a section that rendered none, and lengthens persistent memory's text when the first pass cut it. Every line the first pass rendered stays, in its section and its order, whole if it was whole. An involuntary memory that did not fit its tenth then takes what is still unspent, when it fits that whole.

    A line that shows a memory renders whole or not at all. A retrieved trace, a graph association or the involuntary memory that does not fit whole in what is left of its section is left out, never cut, and the lines after it in that section's order are still tried, so what drops is whole memories: the ones that find no room. Only persistent memory's text is cut to fit, ending with <!-- truncated -->. lineOutcomes reports each trace the block was given a line for, once: included, under the section that shows it, or left_out with its reason, under the first section, in the block's order, that had a line for it. The reason is budget when that section was offered no tokens at all (it is off, or the budget had none) and section_full when the line did not fit whole in what it was offered.

    A trace appears once. A graph association that carries the id of a trace the block already holds (rendered in Relevant Memories or Recent Experiences, or the involuntary memory) is left out of Related Context, and the room goes to the associations after it. In the second pass a trace section leaves out a trace Related Context already shows through its association, and an involuntary memory Related Context shows is not added. As no line leaves the block, a trace an association was left out for stays in the block. tokensUsed counts the headers and separators too and never exceeds totalTokenBudget. The returned allocation holds the shares the sections were granted from, after the defaults, the zeroing and the scaling.

    With recallTraces (retrieval: 'hybrid') the recalled traces are one section, Relevant Memories, in Semantic Recall's place and in the pool's order, granted the recall share (unset: the Semantic Recall and Recent Episodic shares together), and Recent Experiences is not shown. Its lines are reported under recall, and the returned allocation carries that share as recall with semanticRecall and recentEpisodic at 0.

    A retrieved trace with sourceType agent_inference and the tag assistant, the agent's own earlier reply, is shown as an episodic trace whose content starts You said: : in Recent Experiences, or in a hybrid block's one recall section, and reported under the section that shows it. The involuntary memory is shown the same way. Every other trace is shown as it is, an agent_inference trace without the tag among them.
