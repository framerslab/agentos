passage

passage: LibraryPassage

The item's passage that holds the most of the words, then the one of the strongest field, then the earliest; the item's first passage in a listing, or when only its title read from the metadata matched. Its score is the item's rank: for each word, the best of its matches over the item's passages, the field's weight times 1 for the same word, 0.75 for a word it begins and 0.5 for a word one edit away, summed over the words; 0 in a listing.
