Optional maxCharacters

maxCharacters?: number

The most characters of Markdown. The text is cut at the last whole block that fits; past it, a paragraph keeps its words up to a space that fits, a code block the lines that fit with its closing fence, a list the lines that fit, a table its header and the rows that fit, and a quote the blocks that fit with the next one cut by its own rule, however deep the quote sits, each line's quote marks counted. 0, any positive number or Infinity; a bound below 0, NaN or no number at all is refused with a RangeError.

Default

Infinity
