includedMemoryIds

includedMemoryIds: string[]

The ids of the retrieved traces rendered in Relevant Memories and Recent Experiences, then the involuntary memory's when the block shows one. A trace the block shows only through its graph association is not listed here; lineOutcomes reports it as included under graphAssociations.

Optional lineOutcomes

lineOutcomes?: AssembledLineOutcome[]

What became of each line that shows a memory, one entry a trace, in the block's order: the retrieved traces of Relevant Memories and Recent Experiences, the graph associations that carry a trace id, then the involuntary memory. A trace is reported under the section that shows it, so one shown through its association is reported once, under graphAssociations, and an association left out because the block already holds its trace adds no entry. assembleMemoryContext always sets it; a memory manager that builds its context another way may leave it out.

  • Defined in src/cognition/memory/core/types.ts:669

Optional capture

capture?: MemoryRecallCapture

The recall capture of this assembly: every candidate with its ranks, scores and outcome, the legs, the reranker, the budget and the timings, with no memory text and no query text. CognitiveMemoryManager sets it unless its recall.capture is 'off'; a memory manager that builds its context another way may leave it out.
