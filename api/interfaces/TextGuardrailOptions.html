Optional output

output?: "hold" | "stream"

streamText only. hold (default): no text part is delivered before the final guards have judged the whole reply. stream: text flows as it arrives; only guards with evaluateStreamingChunks can stop it mid-stream, and a final block arrives as an error part after the text.
