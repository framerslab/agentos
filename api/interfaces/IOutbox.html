Interface IOutbox

Outbound deliveries: retried with backoff for a day, replayable, content-free after a purge; mail deliveries delivered at most once within their expiry.

enqueue

  • enqueue(delivery, now): Promise<void>

  • Adds a pending delivery, due at now; a repeated id preserves the first delivery and its retry state. After purgeContent for the run, a delivery of a content event (a type in CONTENT_EVENT_TYPES) is not stored: the purge is the outbox's fence. A delivery of any other type (bot:left, bot:purged, bot:done and the rest) is stored. A mail delivery (kind: 'mail') is content: behind the fence it is stored empty and dead, and purgeContent empties it with the content events.

Optional sendClaimed

  • sendClaimed(candidate, send): Promise<boolean>

  • Optional. Sends one delivery under the outbox's own guard: re-reads candidate (a row due returned, which a purge may have emptied since) and, when it is still pending, calls send with the fresh row and a signal that a purge of its run aborts. Answers true when send resolved, false when the delivery was skipped (gone, no longer pending, being sent already, or cancelled by a purge), and rejects with send's error otherwise. OutboxDispatcher sends through it when an outbox has it (InMemoryOutbox, SqlOutbox and FencedOutbox do), and sends the row due returned when it has not.
